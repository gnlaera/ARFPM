Model basis

The engine uses opening-month offset balances for loan-interest calculations, then calculates the closing offset from current month closing cash. Property deductions follow property use and genuine availability for rent, not the property used as loan security. Percentage fields are entered as visible percentages, for example 2.5 means 2.5%. The bonus field is a year 1 cash bonus amount, not a multiplier. The net-worth chart stacks cash, investments, super, property assets and debt so the user can see composition. Novated lease buyout cash is charged in the lease-end month when selected. Stress tests run as a separate comparison case and do not overwrite the base plan.

This is a planning model. It is not a tax return, a regulated financial product recommendation, or personal tax advice. Age Pension settings use the embedded 20 March 2026 single-person parameter basis and should be reviewed when Services Australia updates rates.

Documentation: calculation logic, assumptions and model governance

Model architecture

  • Monthly-first engine from model start to plan end age.
  • Annual tax and summary views aggregate monthly results by Australian financial year.
  • All calculations run locally in the browser. No data is sent to a server.
  • Scenario presets are High Income, Mid Income and Low Income.

Cash flow

  • Salary, bonus, savings interest, dividends, rent, pension, loan proceeds and asset sales are modelled as inflows.
  • Tax withholding, lifestyle costs, private health insurance, property costs, loan interest/principal, novated lease costs and tax settlements are modelled as outflows.
  • If outflows exceed available cash after allowed investment or super bridges, the shortfall becomes overdraft or balancing debt.
  • Surplus cash first supports the cash buffer and can repay overdraft before other allocation.

Tax

  • Resident tax is calculated from financial-year taxable income using embedded tax tables.
  • Low Income Tax Offset, Medicare levy, Medicare levy reduction, Medicare levy surcharge and Division 293 are calculated separately.
  • PAYG withholding is modelled monthly from taxable salary and bonus timing.
  • ATO settlement cash is paid or received in the configured settlement month in the following year.

Property and offset

  • Loan interest uses the opening-month offset balance.
  • Month-end offset is based on current closing cash, capped by excess cash over buffer and relevant loan balance.
  • Cash in offset reduces loan interest once only and does not also earn savings interest.
  • Property deductions follow property use, loan purpose and genuine availability for rent, not loan security.

Super, pension and retirement

  • Employer Superannuation Guarantee, salary sacrifice and personal deductible contributions are modelled as concessional contributions.
  • Post-tax personal super contributions are modelled as personal cash outflows and super inflows. They are not deductible and do not incur concessional-contribution tax.
  • Prior 30 June super is auto-estimated from opening super, model start month and employment/super assumptions. For a 1 July model start it equals opening super.
  • The model assumes the person was employed in the financial year before model start if base salary is positive and the person has not already reached retirement age.
  • Contributions tax, Division 293 and optional cash/super payment treatment are modelled explicitly.
  • Super can be drawn after access age if cash support is needed and the option is enabled.
  • Age Pension uses embedded 20 March 2026 single-person settings for maximum rate, income test, asset test and deeming.
  • The Plan summary shows an estimated annual retirement cash expenditure level that would deplete cash, investments and super to approximately zero by the planning end age, excluding property equity.

Stress testing

  • Stress tests run as a separate case and do not overwrite the base plan.
  • Preset or custom shocks can adjust salary, bonus, rates, inflation, lifestyle costs, asset returns, rental vacancy, retirement age and immediate cash costs.
  • Delta charts show stress impact on net worth, cash, debt and tax settlement by financial year.

Validation checks

  • Monthly closing cash split must tie to savings cash plus offset.
  • Offset cannot exceed available cash above buffer or loan balance.
  • Main-residence property costs are not tax deductions.
  • Closing cash must not become negative after debt bridges.
  • Savings interest is earned only on non-offset savings cash.

Policy settings

  • Default super settings use a 12% Superannuation Guarantee rate, $30,000 concessional cap for FY2026, $32,500 concessional cap from FY2027, $120,000 first-year non-concessional cap, $250,000 Division 293 threshold, 15% Division 293 rate and 20% car statutory FBT rate.
  • These settings are inputs or embedded parameters so they can be reviewed when rules change.

Limitations

  • This is a planning model, not financial advice, tax advice or a tax return engine.
  • Contribution caps are warning checks, not a full excess-contributions tax engine.
  • The retirement spend-to-zero figure is an estimate based on current model assumptions and excludes property equity unless it is sold in the plan.
  • Policy settings must be reviewed after government changes.
  • Rounding, real-world tax timing, asset liquidity, borrowing capacity and individual eligibility can differ from the model.
  • Use exported JSON/CSV files for audit trail and version control.
